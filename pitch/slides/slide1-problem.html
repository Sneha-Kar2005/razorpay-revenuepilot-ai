Revenue is leaking —
quietly, every day.

Failed payments
Declines, expired cards, OTP failures
Retried blindly, or never retried at all.
Abandoned checkouts
Started, never finished
No follow-up, or the same generic nudge for everyone.
Overdue receivables
B2B invoices ageing past due
No structured chaser until someone notices, months later.
